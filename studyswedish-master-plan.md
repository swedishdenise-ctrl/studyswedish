### 1.3 What Each Unit Contains

Every unit follows a consistent structure:
1. **Video Lesson** — Denise teaches the concept (5-15 min), recorded in high quality
2. **Written Lesson** — Full text explanation with examples, tables, tips
3. **Vocabulary List** — Key words with audio pronunciation (Denise's voice), example sentences
4. **Grammar Notes** — Clear, visual grammar explanations with comparison to English
5. **Interactive Exercises** — Fill-in-the-blank, matching, drag-and-drop, multiple choice
6. **Listening Practice** — Audio clips at natural speed + slowed down
7. **Reading Practice** — Short text with comprehension questions
8. **Writing Prompt** — AI-graded writing exercise
9. **Speaking Practice** — Repeat-after-Denise + AI conversation on the unit's topic
10. **Unit Quiz** — Graded assessment, tracked in user dashboard
11. **Cultural Note** — A "Swedish Life" sidebar about relevant culture/traditions

## 2. AI-POWERED FEATURES (The Competitive Edge)

This is where StudySwedish.com becomes something nobody else offers. Every AI feature uses Claude on the backend.

### 2.1 AI Swedish Tutor ("Fråga Denise" — Ask Denise)
- Conversational AI that speaks Swedish at the user's level
- Adjusts vocabulary and grammar complexity based on user's current level
- Can explain grammar rules in English when asked
- Roleplay scenarios: at the store, job interview, making friends, at the doctor
- Corrects mistakes in real-time with gentle explanations
- Remembers context within the conversation
- Text-based initially, voice in v2

### 2.2 AI Grammar Checker
- Paste or type Swedish text
- Get corrections with explanations of WHY it's wrong
- Shows the grammar rule being violated
- Links to relevant lesson for deeper learning
- Rates text by CEFR level

### 2.3 AI Writing Coach
- Writing prompts at every level
- Submit writing → get detailed feedback
- Feedback covers: grammar, vocabulary, style, fluency, coherence
- Suggests improvements with before/after examples
- Tracks writing improvement over time

### 2.4 AI Pronunciation Coach (v2)
- Uses Web Speech API for speech recognition
- User reads a phrase → AI evaluates pronunciation
- Highlights problem sounds
- Shows mouth position diagrams
- Side-by-side comparison with Denise's pronunciation

### 2.5 AI-Personalized Learning Path
- Placement test on signup determines starting level
- AI tracks performance across all exercises
- Identifies weak areas (e.g., "you struggle with past tense irregular verbs")
- Recommends specific lessons and practice
- Weekly progress emails with AI-generated study plan
- Adapts difficulty in real-time

### 2.6 AI Sentence Builder
- User selects: topic, grammar point, vocabulary set
- AI generates unlimited practice sentences
- Each sentence comes with: translation, grammar breakdown, audio
- Never runs out of practice material

### 2.7 AI Translation Helper
- Not just translate — EXPLAIN
- Shows literal translation, natural translation, and grammar breakdown
- "Why is it said this way?" explanations
- Highlights common English→Swedish interference errors

### 2.8 AI Story Generator
- AI creates short stories at the user's level
- Stories use vocabulary from recently studied units
- Comprehension questions after each story
- Vocabulary highlighted and clickable for definitions

## 5. DESIGN DIRECTION

### 5.1 Design Philosophy: Scandinavian Luxury Meets Modern Tech

The design should feel like a premium Scandinavian brand — think the love child of Spotify's interface and a high-end Stockholm design studio. Clean, warm, confident, with moments of delight.

**Core Principles:**
- **Lagom** — Not too much, not too little. Balanced.
- **Light & Warmth** — Bright, airy, inviting. Not cold minimalism.
- **Nature-Inspired** — Subtle textures, organic shapes, Swedish landscape colors
- **Personality** — Denise's warmth and humor comes through in copy and micro-interactions
- **Premium Feel** — Every detail considered. Nothing generic.

### 5.2 Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Primary | Swedish Blue | `#005B99` | Headers, CTAs, navigation |
| Secondary | Golden Yellow | `#FFCB05` | Accents, highlights, achievements |
| Warm White | Cream | `#FAF7F2` | Backgrounds |
| Text | Deep Charcoal | `#1A1A2E` | Body text |
| Success | Forest Green | `#2D6A4F` | Correct answers, progress |
| Accent | Soft Coral | `#E07A5F` | Notifications, alerts, warmth |
| Neutral | Warm Gray | `#8B8B8B` | Secondary text, borders |

### 5.3 Typography

- **Display / Headings**: A distinctive serif or semi-serif (think: Fraunces, Playfair Display, or Libre Caslon) — editorial, warm, Scandinavian feel
- **Body**: A clean, highly readable sans-serif (think: Satoshi, General Sans, or Cabinet Grotesk) — modern but warm
- **Code/Grammar Examples**: Monospace for linguistic examples (JetBrains Mono or similar)
- **Swedish Text Examples**: Slightly larger, highlighted with the golden accent

### 5.4 UI Components & Patterns

- **Lesson Cards**: Rounded corners, subtle shadows, progress indicator, level badge
- **Quiz Interface**: Full-screen, distraction-free, animated feedback (confetti on correct streaks)
- **AI Chat**: Floating panel, Denise's avatar, message bubbles, typing indicator
- **Progress Dashboard**: Visual progress bars, streak counter, level map (think: a path through Swedish landscapes)
- **Navigation**: Clean top nav, sticky on scroll, mobile hamburger with full-screen overlay
- **Dark Mode**: Full support, switches to deep navy/charcoal palette

### 5.5 Key Design Moments

1. **First Visit**: Animated hero with Denise's welcome, Swedish flag colors sweep, "Start Your Journey" CTA
2. **Completing a Lesson**: Satisfying animation, streak counter increments, XP gained
3. **Level Up**: Full-screen celebration, certificate preview, shareable badge
4. **AI Chat**: Feels like texting a friend, not using a chatbot
5. **Loading States**: Swedish-themed skeleton screens with fun Swedish facts

## 6. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

### 6.1 Stack

| Layer | Technology | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Frontend | Next.js (App Router) | SSR for SEO, React ecosystem, Vercel deploy |

### 6.3 Row Level Security (RLS) Strategy

- Users can only read/write their own progress, vocabulary, exercise attempts, and conversations
- Lessons and grammar reference: readable by all, writable by admin only
- Free lessons: filtered by `is_free = true` for non-premium users
- Blog posts: readable by all (public SEO content)
- AI conversation message count enforced via edge function (5/day for free, unlimited for premium)

### 6.4 API Architecture

- **Supabase Auth**: Email/password + Google OAuth + magic links
- **Edge Functions**: AI endpoints (tutor, grammar check, writing feedback), rate limiting, Stripe webhooks
- **Realtime**: Community features, live Q&A chat
- **Storage**: Audio files (Denise's recordings), user uploads (writing submissions), images
