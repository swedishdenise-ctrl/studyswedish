# Audio Narration Script
## 50 Svenska Ordspråk — StudySwedish.com

**Recording tips:**
- Find a quiet room, close the door and windows
- Record on your phone (Voice Memos app on iPhone works great) or your computer
- Hold the phone about 15–20 cm from your mouth
- Speak at a natural, calm pace — slightly slower than conversation
- Say each proverb TWICE so learners can follow along

---

### INTRO (read once, at the start)

"Hej och välkommen. I'm Denise from StudySwedish.com, and this is the audio companion to 50 Svenska Ordspråk — 50 Swedish Proverbs. I'll read each proverb twice, slowly and clearly, so you can follow along in your ebook. Let's begin."

*[short pause — 2 seconds]*

---
